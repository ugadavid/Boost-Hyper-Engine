# BHE-BE Laboratory

## Why This Branch Exists

`experiment/bhe-be` exists as a **real-world stress-test laboratory** for BHE.

Its purpose is to confront BHE abstractions with a complete pedagogical ecosystem already in use:

* real teachers;
* real learners;
* real pedagogical constraints;
* real sequencing choices;
* real successes and failures.

Boost’English is used here as:

> a situated pedagogical corpus.

Not as a target product.

Not as a model to reproduce.

Not as a hidden specification for BHE.

The objective is simple:

```txt
pressure-test ideas
before stabilizing architecture
```

---

## What BHE-BE Is

BHE-BE is:

* a case study;
* an observation space;
* an experimentation branch;
* a controlled environment for architectural emergence.

The branch exists to help answer questions such as:

```txt
What survives contact with reality?
What repeatedly emerges?
What breaks?
What appears useful?
What is merely contextual?
```

The goal is not to confirm hypotheses.

The goal is to challenge them.

---

## What BHE-BE Is Not

BHE-BE is **not**:

* a Boost’English clone;
* a pedagogical truth machine;
* a hidden roadmap for BHE;
* a replacement for the main architecture.

Very important principle:

> Boost’English may nourish BHE — or not.

Some ideas may survive and deserve integration.

Others may reveal themselves to be too context-dependent or too specific to Boost’English.

This is expected.

Failure is useful.

---

## Working Hypotheses (Experimental)

The following hypotheses are currently under observation.

They are **not stabilized concepts**.

### 1. Cognitive operations are composable

A single pedagogical object may mobilize several learner cognitive operations at once.

Example:

```txt
notice
+
compare
+
produce
```

This hypothesis is partially stabilized in the core through the lightweight `CognitiveOperation` vocabulary.

---

### 2. Orchestration may matter more than exercise type

The pedagogical value of a sequence may depend less on isolated activities and more on:

```txt
how things are articulated
```

This remains under observation.

No universal sequence model is assumed.

---

### 3. `Moment` may emerge as an orchestration object (?)

Possible hypothesis:

```txt
Moment
=
pedagogical orchestration unit
```

Potentially containing:

* cognitive intentions;
* interaction modes;
* renderers;
* teacher mediation;
* sequencing role.

Important:

This is **not a conclusion**.

This branch exists partly to determine whether this idea survives repeated confrontation with real cases.

---

### 4. Teacher orchestration matters

“For teachers” sections suggest that important pedagogical intelligence may partially live:

```txt
outside learner-facing activities
```

This remains exploratory.

---

### 5. Some evaluations are not evaluations

Certain activities may resemble quizzes while actually serving:

```txt
reflection
metacognition
positioning
awareness
```

rather than scoring.

This distinction may become important later.

---

## Method

The branch follows a strict discipline:

```txt
observe
↓
decompose
↓
pressure-test
↓
stabilize
↓
only then consider integration into core
```

No concept enters `main` simply because it appears elegant.

Ideas must survive confrontation with reality.

---

## Relationship With Main

`main` remains:

```txt
stable
minimal
carefully generalized
```

`experiment/bhe-be` exists to explore.

The responsibility of this branch is:

> think dangerously without contaminating the core.
